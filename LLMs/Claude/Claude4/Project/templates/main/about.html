Who We Are

FC Ironclad was founded in 2013 by a group of friends who believed football was more than a game — it was a way to build community, foster discipline, and create lasting bonds.

Over the years we've grown from a Sunday kickabout into a fully organised club with youth academies, senior teams, and a passionate supporter base.

Our Values

  • Respect — for teammates, opponents, and referees.
  • Resilience — we never stop fighting until the final whistle.
  • Unity — one club, one family.
  • Development — every player grows here.
